### What I Build & Research

- **Audio & Deep Learning:** Designing parameter-shared Transformers and CNNs in PyTorch for Automatic Chord Recognition, tackling long-tailed distributions and uncertainty-driven Active Learning at **Moises AI**.
- **LLM Safety & Multimodal AI:** Benchmarking guardrails against jailbreaks and prompt injection, and orchestrating low-latency Vision-Language Models for real-time intent detection.
- **Data & Predictive Systems:** Building Medallion medical imaging pipelines with OCR de-biasing and industrial time-series anomaly/RUL models.
